Product Design · B2B SaaS · Messaging

Noviopus

Messaging experience for a careers platform where candidates and employers talk directly. I designed the flow end to end for desktop and mobile: composing a message, categorised inboxes, time-period filtering and the overlay menus that hold it together — delivered as high-fidelity screens covering every state.

Role

UX/UI Designer

Engagement

Volunteer · Remote

Year

2023 – 2025

Platforms

Desktop & Mobile

01 · The surface

Three columns, always in the same place

The three columns never move. Whatever you open, the anchors stay where your eye left them — so the product never has to be re-read.

02 · Categories

One inbox, five contexts

New, Starred, Private, Work and Jobs are not five screens. They are one screen with a different list — so moving between them costs the user nothing to relearn.

03 · Composing

Writing without losing your place

Compose replaces the conversation list with a recipient picker and leaves the thread where it was, so starting a new message never clears the screen you were reading.

04 · Filtering

Finding a message by when it happened

Filtering opens from the thread header instead of a separate search screen, so narrowing a long conversation never costs you the conversation.

05 · Mobile

The same model at 360px

The three columns collapse to one and the navigation becomes an overlay. Nothing is dropped — the structure is the same one, folded.
