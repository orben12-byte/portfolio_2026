Product Design · Graduation Project · Mobile App

Life Hero

Most habit apps ask you to tick a box. Life Hero asks you to become someone: a comic-book hero who ranks up every time you show up, loses fights, collects costumes, and has a boss waiting at the end of the week.

Role

UX & UI, end to end

Type

Graduation project

Year

2023

Platform

Mobile

01 · Zeigarnik Effect

Locked, half-collected, two ranks below

Achievements that have not opened yet. Nine costumes with most of them still grey. A league that puts two heroes above you. Three different ways to be almost there.

02 · Peak-End Rule

The week has a boss

Bad Apple, level 2, on a 24-hour timer. Every challenge you finish lands a hit you can watch. The week ends on a won fight and thirty experience points.

03 · Creating a habit

Three questions, asked by the hero

A name, a difficulty, a summary. The same three fields a form would have, asked one at a time by a character who waits for the answer.

04 · The first run

You lose the first fight on purpose

The devil is your boss at work. You attack, you miss, you die, and the screen says so in red. The sidekick arrives after that, and only then does setup start.

05 · When it breaks

Your Page Is in Another Castle

The 404 is a joke the audience will get. The delete says what you lose before you lose it. The sign-in marks which field was wrong instead of failing silently.

06 · The system

One tab bar, every state it has

The plus opens into three actions, so the bar is drawn in all of them. The mission card has its own sheet, the milestone tiles theirs, locked and unlocked.

Scope

What this never became

A graduation project: designed in full, never built. The psychology here is reasoning about how habits form, not a result measured on anyone.
